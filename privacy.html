Privacy Policy

This is a personal Google Drive integration operated by its owner using rclone.

Data Access

The application accesses the owner's Google Drive to upload and manage files as authorized by the owner.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is used only for the owner's personal file management and synchronization.

Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally share Google Drive data with third parties. Data is transferred between the owner's computer and Google Drive.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. Uploaded files are stored in Google Drive.

Revoking Access

Access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account security settings.
